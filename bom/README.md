# Bill of materials

## 📋 Full structured BOM with costs

The complete structured BOM of the AC/BC rotary table — every assembly and every single part, exported directly from the CAD model, with all costs. The cost of an assembly is the sum of its parts; the total of the table is at the bottom (**$147.40**).

- **[Interactive view on masdrive](https://masdrive.ch/myprojects/projects/ac-table/#stueckliste)** — the nicest way to browse, with thumbnails
- **[Markdown view in this repo](ac-table-structured-bom.md)** — collapsible, generated from the Excel file
- **[Excel download](ABC%20Rotary%20Table%20-%20structured%20BOM%20with%20costs.xlsx)** — grouped rows to expand and collapse (original: [ac-tisch-stueckliste-mehrstufig.xlsx](https://masdrive.ch/myprojects/assets/media/2026/10/ac-tisch-stueckliste-mehrstufig.xlsx))
